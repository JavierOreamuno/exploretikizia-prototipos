The hard part

Four institutions, one mountain

SINAC issues the permit. CRC Chirripó runs the base camp. ATURENA covers the San Jerónimo sector. Knowing who does what is half the battle.

Issues the permit

SINAC

The national parks authority. Online reservation only, quarterly release, 52 places a day.

Runs the base camp

CRC Chirripó

Beds, meals and porters at Crestones. Booked separately from the park permit.

San Jerónimo sector

ATURENA

The quieter route has a different booking body altogether. Most guides skip this one.

Where we come in

You

Chirripó permits are issued by SINAC, Costa Rica’s national parks authority, with a limited daily quota released quarterly. Explore Tikizia helps you understand and navigate that process. We do not control availability and cannot guarantee a permit.
