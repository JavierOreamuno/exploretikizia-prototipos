Grading

One scale across every kind of day.

A boat trip and a mountain traverse belong on the same axis, or the number means nothing.

  1. 01

    The grade is effort, not activity

    Grade 1 is a day anyone who can walk twenty minutes can take — that covers the sloth walk, the wetland boat and the shortest forest loop alike. Grade 5 is the traverse.

  2. 02

    Relative to our own catalogue, not the country

    “Moderate” means nothing on its own. The five bars place each product against everything else we sell, so the comparison is internal and checkable.

  3. 03

    Walked, floated or paddled before it is listed

    A category opens when we have run every product in it ourselves. That is why they launch one at a time instead of all at once.

  4. 04

    Times are door to door

    Including the drive, the stops and the queue at the gate — not just the hours you are moving.

Questions

Before you book.

Why can I only book hiking right now?

Because that is the category we have finished measuring. A category goes live when every product in it has been run by us and graded — the rest are on the table above with their phase marked, not hidden.

What does the grade mean on a boat trip?

The same thing it means on a trail: effort, not activity. Grade 1 is a day anyone who can walk twenty minutes can take. That is why the wetland boat and the shortest forest loop share a number.

How are tours organised?

On three facts: location, experience and grade. A tour lives at one flat address and appears under whichever destination and category claim it, so opening Nature & Wildlife never moves an existing URL.

What are the guides?

A second product line. Each destination gets one $10 digital guide with the full dataset, sold and delivered here. The packing list is free and works everywhere.

Do I pay now or on the day?

Now, by card. That is what holds the place. Full refund up to 48 hours before departure. Final wording pending the client.
