Timetable

Door to door, four hours.

The cone is usually clear until around ten and clouded by early afternoon. Every time below is set by that one fact.

  1. 08:00

    Pick-up

    Your hotel lobby in La Fortuna. Fifteen minutes to the gate on Route 142, then signposted gravel.

  2. 08:30

    El Ceibo · 1.8 km

    Flat, shaded, secondary forest. The scope comes out here.

  3. 10:00

    Lava flow · 2.6 km

    Loose black rock, no shade. Closest approach to the cone.

  4. 11:15

    Lake viewpoint

    Last on purpose — by now the volcano has usually gone.

  5. 12:00

    Back in town

    Dropped where you were collected.

Stated plainly

Two things the brochures leave out.

  • You will not see lava. Arenal stopped erupting in 2010; the flow is cold rock.
  • The cone is often invisible. It makes its own weather, and no operator can promise you a view of it.
