Requirements

Who this route is for.

Suitable if

  • You want the valley, the hot springs and the scenery without the summit push
  • Your SINAC permit application did not go through this quarter
  • You are travelling with people who should not attempt the full ascent

Not suitable if

  • Reaching the 3,820 m summit is the whole point of your trip — book the summit trek instead
  • You need technical climbing or rappelling — this route has neither

Practical

Everything before you book.

  1. 01

    Departure

    Early morning from San Gerardo de Rivas. Exact pick-up time confirmed right after booking.

  2. 02

    Meeting point

    Hotel pick-up in San Gerardo de Rivas or San Isidro del General. Ask before booking if you are staying further out.

  3. 03

    Group

    Shared departure. Private departures available — ask on the itinerary form.

  4. 04

    Cancellation

    Full refund up to 48 hours before departure. Wording is a placeholder pending the client.
