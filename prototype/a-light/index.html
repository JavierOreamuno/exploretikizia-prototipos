Questions

Before you book.

Am I fit enough for these?

Five of the eight are under 8 km with less than 400 m of climb — a normal walking day. The Fortuna → Monteverde traverse is 17 km and 900 m, and we ask about your recent walking before confirming it.

What happens if it rains?

We walk. This is rainforest and it rains most afternoons for half the year. We only cancel for conditions that make a trail unsafe — and then you choose another date or take a full refund, not a credit note.

Do I pay now or on the day?

Now, by card, through BAC Credomatic. That is what holds the seat. Full refund up to 48 hours before departure. Final cancellation wording pending the client.

How are tours organised?

On two axes: location and experience. A tour lives at one flat address and appears under whichever destination and experience claim it, so opening Monteverde or adding Nature & Wildlife never moves an existing URL.

What are the guides?

A second product line. Each destination gets one $10 digital guide, sold and delivered here. The packing list is free and works everywhere, which is why it is the one we give away.
