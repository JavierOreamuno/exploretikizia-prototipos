Things to do in
La Fortuna, Costa Rica.

The volcano, the waterfall, the bridges and the wildlife — what each one costs, how long it takes, and whether you actually need a guide for it. Written by people who live three hours away and go often.

  • Attractions5 within an hour
  • Tours here12 · from $63
  • Stay3–4 nights
  • SeasonYear round

Four days is the honest answer.

Most itineraries give La Fortuna two nights, and most people leave having seen the volcano from a car park. The attractions below are spread across a 60-minute radius, and two of them — Río Celeste and Caño Negro — are day trips in their own right.

Nothing on this page requires a tour. Everything on this page can be done with one, and we say which is which.

Four days

An order that does not waste a morning

Built around drive times and opening hours, not around what we would like to sell you.

  1. Day 1

    Arenal Volcano National Park

    Arrive by 08:00 for the clearest view of the cone — it clouds over most afternoons. El Ceibo trail, then the 1968 lava flow.

  2. Day 2

    Waterfall, then the bridges

    Waterfall first while your legs are fresh; the 500 steps are worse on the way up. Bridges in the afternoon, when the heat drops under the canopy.

  3. Day 3

    Río Celeste

    Ninety minutes north, and a full day once you are there. Leave at 06:00 — the park caps daily entries and turns people away.

  4. Day 4

    Caño Negro, or a slow morning

    The wetlands if you want wildlife, hot springs if four days of walking was enough.
