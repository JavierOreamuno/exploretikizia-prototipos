Before you book

Is this the right day for you?

Difficulty

Easy 1 of 5

Flat forest trail on the El Ceibo loop, then loose volcanic rock on the lava flow. No climbing, no rappelling, no altitude.

  • You can walk 3–4 km on flat ground, some of it on uneven rock
  • Good for first-timers and for families with children who walk unaided
  • You want the wildlife — the guide carries the scope, and that is the whole difference
  • You will not see lava. Arenal stopped erupting in 2010 and the flow is cold rock.
  • The cone clouds over most afternoons. We depart in the morning for that reason, and we still cannot promise you a view.
