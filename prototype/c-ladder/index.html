Two kinds of tour, told apart on purpose

Instant confirmation means it is our own departure: you book and pay in one step and the seat is yours. On request means the tour is run by a partner operator: you send the request, we confirm the space with them, and only then do we send you the payment link. You never pay before a seat is confirmed.

Common questions

Answered before you pay, not after

Is this a full itinerary planning service?

No. The $100 consultation is a Q&A-style session, ideal for DIY travellers who want expert insight without hiring a full planner. Full planning is a separate service, quoted after a short call.

Can I reschedule if something comes up?

Yes — just let us know at least 24 hours in advance. The full cancellation and refund policy is shown at checkout, before you pay.

Do I need to prepare anything?

Yes, please fill out the short intake form with your draft itinerary or your questions. It takes about two minutes.

Can you guarantee a Chirripó permit?

No. Permits are issued by SINAC with a limited daily quota released quarterly. We help you understand and navigate the process; we don’t control availability.
